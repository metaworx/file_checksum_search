# AP Vocabulary v1.2: the last of "definition"

> **Status: approved 2026-09-02.** Follows v1.1 (committed as `c829fb0`,
> `0c0095a`, `f30faea`), which renamed the ids and left these behind.

## Discussion

v1.1 scoped `definition` to the three button ids and declared the rest out of
scope, because two of the remaining occurrences are user-visible text and the
AP claimed to touch no shipped behaviour. Those two, and the internal names
behind them, are what is left.

In both cases the admin page is the outlier and its personal twin is already
right, so nothing here needs a convention invented for it.

| | Admin (now) | Personal (already) |
|---|---|---|
| Delete confirm | `Delete this rule definition?` | `Delete this rule?` |
| Composable's list | `definitions` | `rules` |
| Composable's error | `definitionsError` | `error` |
| Composable's loader | `loadDefinitions` | `loadRules` |

`useAdminSettings` passes every other key of `useRules()` straight through
(`supportedAlgos: rules.supportedAlgos`, and eight more). These three are the
only ones it renames on the way out, and it renames them to a word the model
dropped. Removing the rename is the whole change.

## Analysis

### User-visible

| Location | Now | After |
|---|---|---|
| `settings-admin-vue/App.vue:400` | `<h4>Rule Definitions</h4>` | `<h4>Rules</h4>` |
| `settings-admin-vue/App.vue:201` | `Delete this rule definition?` | `Delete this rule?` |

Neither is wrapped in a translation call, so there is no l10n impact. Nothing
in the e2e or vitest suites asserts on either string.

`Rules` sits next to `Rule Editing Permission` in the same page, and the hint
directly under it already says what the section is for.

### Internal

| Location | Now | After |
|---|---|---|
| `useAdminSettings.ts:101` | `definitions: rules.rules` | `rules: rules.rules` |
| `useAdminSettings.ts:111` | `definitionsError: rules.error` | `error: rules.error` |
| `useAdminSettings.ts:113` | `loadDefinitions: rules.load` | `loadRules: rules.load` |
| `App.vue:34,37,59,243,414` | `definitions`, `loadDefinitions` | `rules`, `loadRules` |
| `useAdminSettings.spec.ts:74` | `loadDefinitions()` | `loadRules()` |

`definitionsError` is exported and never consumed. It is renamed rather than
deleted, because the twin exposes `error` too and removing public surface is a
different decision from renaming it.

No shadowing: inside the composable the `useRules('all')` result is the local
`rules`, and `rules: rules.rules` is a property key, not a binding.
`toRefs(state)` contributes `status`, `statusLoading`, `statusError` and
`lastUpdated`, so the new `error` collides with nothing.

### What stays

`rules-vue/types.ts:6` names the removed `DefinitionData`/`PersonalRule`
interfaces in a comment about what superseded them. It is an accurate statement
about the past; renaming it would make it false.

Backend `RuleDefinitionValidator`, `RuleDefinition` and the `array $definition`
parameters in `docs/api-v1.md` remain out of scope, as in v1.1.
