# AP Nc35 v1.0: Nextcloud 35 support

## Discussion

- **The ask.** The user, 2026-09-30: support Nextcloud 35.
- **Not scheduled.** It is a brief plan.

## Analysis

**Today:**
- `appinfo/info.xml` declares Nextcloud 33 to 34 (`max-version="34"`).
- CI's PHPUnit and Cypress matrices test `stable33` and `stable34`.
- The harness already runs Nextcloud 35.0.1 (instance 35).

**Unknown until measured:**
- whether the unit, integration and e2e suites pass on 35;
- what Nextcloud 35 deprecated or removed among the OCP interfaces and
  events the app uses, per its developer manual's upgrade notes;
- whether `@nextcloud/vue` and the other frontend libraries need a newer
  version for 35.

## Implementation Plan

1. **Measure.** Run the PHPUnit suites and the e2e specs on instance 35;
   read the upgrade notes against the app's use of OCP. Write down what
   fails.
2. **Fix** what fails, each defect as a commit of its own with its tests,
   while 33 and 34 keep passing.
3. **Declare it.**
   - `max-version="35"` in `appinfo/info.xml`;
   - `stable35` in CI's PHPUnit and Cypress matrices;
   - the README's supported versions;
   - a CHANGELOG bullet.

**Verification:** every suite on 33, 34 and 35; the manifest schema.
