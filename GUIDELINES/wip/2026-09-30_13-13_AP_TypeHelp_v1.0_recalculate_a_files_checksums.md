# AP TypeHelp v1.0: recalculate a file's checksums

## Discussion

- The user, 2026-09-30: "[FIX] "recalculate a file by hand" wording",
  after the British English and the Dutch and Polish reviews found that the
  rule type's help recalculates the file, not its checksums.

## Analysis

The help of the rule dialog's Type field (`src/rules-vue/RuleForm.vue:246`)
says "Ignore" "still lets someone recalculate a file by hand". What is
recalculated is a file's checksums, as the other texts say it ("Recalculating
checksums by hand", "may recalculate checksums by hand"). The wording was in
v0.20.3 and survived the English rework.

It is one text, in no spec, test or document. Its translations:

- **Already right:** Polish ("przeliczyć sumy kontrolne pliku").
- **Right, but singular:** Dutch ("de controlesom van een bestand").
- **Recalculate the file:** de, de_DE, es, fr, it, ja, pt_BR, zh_CN.
- **The English itself:** en_GB.

The CHANGELOG's Changed bullet on the texts ("help texts that say what the
app does") already covers it.
