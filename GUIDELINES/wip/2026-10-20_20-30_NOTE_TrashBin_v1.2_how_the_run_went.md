# NOTE TrashBin v1.2: how the run went

Blocks 2 to 8, 2026-10-20, under `PLAN++ D5-A+B`.

## Where the run went differently

- **Block 4:**
  `withVersionAreas()` and `withVersionFiles()` became
  `withPlacedAreas()` and `withPlaces()`, since they place trashed files too.
  A team folder's trash rows, which no prefix kept out,
  had been listed unplaced for a reach of every file;
  they are now placed, or left out where their record is gone.
- **Block 6:**
  the queue could not open a team folder's trashed file,
  which the root folder does not reach.
  `FilecacheService` now asks the trash app (`ITrashManager::getTrashNodeById()`),
  as the account `group_folders_trash.deleted_by` names;
  an older team folders app keeps no deleter, and such a file stays unread.
- **Block 6:**
  the rule shape in `docs/api-v1.md` and `docs/api-v1-openapi.yaml`
  got `trash` with the switch rather than in Block 8,
  since the changelog bullet points there.
- **Block 7:**
  `--mark` queues the files in the trash whose rule says *Trash*,
  as it queues versions; the plan named only `--trash`'s refusal.
- **Block 8:**
  the blank line between two `[Unreleased]` bullets,
  missed in Block 6, was put in.

## Not covered

- `fcias:hash` pages a home's `files_versions` and `files_trashbin/files`:
  a version of a trashed file, at `files_trashbin/versions`, is in neither pass.
