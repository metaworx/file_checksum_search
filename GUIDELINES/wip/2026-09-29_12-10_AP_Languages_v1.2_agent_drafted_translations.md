## Analysis

- **Team folders' label and name** (unchanged from v1.1):
  `src/rules-vue/bands.ts:131` joins `` `${groupFolderTerm}: ${name}` ``
  outside `t()`; `GroupFolderService::appName()` reads the app's name
  without a language, where core translates an app's name through that
  app's catalogue (`NavigationManager`: `IFactory::get($app)->t(...)`).
- **English that misled translators:**
  - The Reset hint, "disowned by a reset — already hidden from search, and
    the background job clears them as it goes" (`settings-admin-vue/App.vue:161`).
    A reset (`occ fcias:reset --hashes`) marks stored hashes `stale:reset`;
    the queue drain (`ProcessPendingUpdates` →
    `RuleService::clearDisownedFiles`) deletes them. "Disowned" was
    translated as "orphaned", the orphan purge's word, which deletes the
    entries of files that no longer exist.
  - "Search {folders}…" (`RuleForm.vue:212`), the placeholder of the picker
    that selects the one team folder a rule addresses; three drafts
    translated it as searching inside the folders. Its comment belongs to
    the line above.
  - "Search anywhere" (`DuplicateListing.vue:369`), the switch that lets the
    typed hash match mid-string; read as "search in every place". The hash
    help text names it and says "Tick" for a switch.
  - "Min" and "Limit", the smallest group to list and the groups per page,
    side by side: read as one quantity's bounds.
  - The Priority column's help (`RuleTable.vue:85`) describes the cells'
    notation as `"<band>.<position>"`; the cells show `7.1`, the words are
    never replaced, and "keep it as it is" made reviewers think it was a
    literal.
- **German corrections** (reviews): type, not mode, for Include / Ignore /
  Exclude (banner and glossary note; the labels stay translated); Recalc
  "Berechnen" under the heading "Neu berechnen".
