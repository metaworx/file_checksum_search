## Implementation Plan

1. **[FIX] three code fixes**, each a commit of its own with its tests,
   German and CHANGELOG bullet:
   - the Duplicates page hides "Verify" and "Verify all" from an account
     that may not recalculate, as the sidebar does (A2);
   - the New rule dialog and "Create rule" pre-fill the default algorithm
     (A5);
   - the personal-rule refusal goes through `IL10N`, saying "team folder".
   **Verification:** unit and integration tests, Vitest, ESLint, ECS,
   `l10n.sh check`.
2. **[FIX] the English says what the app does**: every finding of the
   analysis as decided, with `TRANSLATORS` comments; the user guide and the
   docs follow; the German follows. One commit.
   **Verification:** `l10n.sh check`, Vitest, ESLint, the manifest schema.
3. **German delta review**, by the German reviewer, of the texts step 2
   changed; its findings to a gate, the accepted ones into step 2's commit.
4. **The eight languages**, one agent each in its worktree: fast-forward,
   `update`, translate every new or changed text (their `next.po` where it
   applies), the new summary and description with the machine-translation
   note in the language and in English; checks pass.
5. **Delta reviews**, each language's earlier reviewer, of the texts step 4
   changed; findings applied.
6. **Integrate**, one commit per language: `.po`, glossary, `l10n/`,
   `info.xml` (`fr`, `es`, `it`, `nl`, `ja`, `pl`, `pt-br`, `zh-hans`),
   CHANGELOG Added.
7. **British English** (`en_GB`): the English with British spelling and
   usage ("authorisation", "Re-apply"), one commit.
8. Remove the worktrees and their branches.
